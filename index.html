Welcome to the L5R Oracle of the Void Database

After Legend of the Five Rings was sold to Fantasy Flight at the end of 2015, Don Eisele (Author of the Oracle of the Void) has continued to fund and run the Oracle of the Void. He has revamped the Oracle into a serverless cloud architecture on AWS. The new version is faster, more resilient, has more features, and supports other games.
