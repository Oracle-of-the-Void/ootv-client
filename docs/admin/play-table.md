# Play table

Last updated: 2026-11-06 (l5r/play version 20; structured card effects; Ranged Attacks and Fear; End without resolving; Reactions; Honor actions; into play, tokens, take control; Destroy; Force, Chi, Province Strength; draw, discard, look, search; move, remove from battle, send home, assign; copy, rule changes; Water Dragon's Events Phase wait; bow, straighten, dishonor; Gold and costs; Cavalry; Terrain swaps, l5r/fields version 28)

The play table (`/play/`, repo `ootv-play`; on preview only so far) plays a game from its table config: the
zones, the layout rows, setup, card states, counters, the turn's phases and the attack, and the rules that
change by arc. The live site reads it from the settings (`oracle-all-structure`), item **`<game>/play`**; L5R's
is `l5r/play`, created 2026-11-03 at version 1, now version 19. There's no Admin editor for it yet.

## Where it comes from

- `ootv-play` `games/<game>.json` is the working copy. Preview and local copies of the table play by that file,
  so rules can be tried there without touching the settings; `?config=structure` on the table's address makes
  preview play by the item instead.
- Before a release, the item is brought up to date from the file, one key at a time on the live version (ootv-tools
  `structure/set.py`). `ootv-play` `test/structure-diff.mjs` lists the keys that differ.
- `l5r/play` is kept in step with `games/l5r.json` as the table's work is merged (the user, 2026-11-03), one changed
  path at a time. Version 2 (2026-11-03) added `tokens`, `table.arrange` and the provinces' `attach`, and moved
  `placement` to card widths. Version 3 (2026-11-03): Gold only from Ivory on (`counters` per arc, `clear: phase`). Version 4 (2026-11-03): battles (each arc's `phases[].attack`: Maneuvers segments `assign`, the Resolution step `resolve`, `commit`, `honor`). Version 5 (2026-11-03): Shinsei's Shrine keeps its province empty (the provinces' `norefill`; `title` among the `cardfields`). Version 6 (2026-11-03): the Events Phase turns provinces up one at a time and waits at each Event (the Events phase `reveal`, which also attaches Regions); tokens by name (`cardcounters` +1F, +1C, +1F/+1C, -1F; `customtokens`; `tokenforce` counts a token's F in battle); dishonored cards turn upside down (`cardstates` look `turn`); Arrange: Standard (`table.arrange.standard`). Version 7 (2026-11-03): the provinces' `norefill` title rule is gone; Shinsei's Shrine's own card field `play` says it (`cardfields` gains `play`; see below). Version 8 (2026-11-03): Gold (`gold`: bowing a card with Gold Production adds it; a card bought from a province in the Dynasty Phase pays its cost and the rest is lost; a Personality of the player's clan paid in full adds its Personal Honor, else costs 2 less; a Gold counter before Ivory too; Ivory and Three Dynasties keep a pool with no clan rule), `cardfields` cost, production, conditionalvalues, clan, ph; who goes first (`firstplayer`: the highest starting Honor, a tie at random, SCC p. 58); seats are Player A and Player B. Version 9 (2026-11-03): Province 5 and 6 (`zones[].extra`), out of the game until a player picks Add a province; every province list (`table.rows`, `gold.buy.from`, the Events `reveal`/`faceup` zones, `attack.battlefields`, in each arc) names them. Version 10 (2026-11-04): `stats` (Force, Chi), the stats a card's menu can change by hand for a while (until the end of the turn, the battle, or taken off). Version 11 (2026-11-04): `table.offscreen`, the colours of the small triangles that point at cards out of view (`clan`: by the unit's main card's clan; `type`: Holdings white, Regions and Events tan; `default`). Version 12 (2026-11-04): `victory`, winning and losing: 40 Family Honor as a player's turn begins wins; a player with no provinces left, or at –20 Family Honor, is out (`dishonorat`: at once up to Emperor, at the end of their own turn from Ivory on, in `arcrules` Ivory and Three Dynasties, as each arc's book says); five Rings of different elements in play wins; the last player in wins. Version 13 (2026-11-04): the active area (a zone `active`, area `active`, layout `strip`: cards with something going on that aren't in play, an action being resolved or a lasting Event, left of each play area); `handplay`, playing a hand card: an Item, Follower, Spell or Ancestor attaches to the card it's played on, paid for; anything else goes to the active area, on its target if it has one; `actions`, how the table finds a card's actions in its text (a bold label ending in a colon with a timing word: Battle, Open, Limited, ...) and names the icons in them (`:bow:` Bow, `:g1:` 1 Gold), for the "Play / Use … on …?" questions. Version 14 (2026-11-04): duels, by Scorpion Clan Coup pp. 54–55 (`duel`: who can duel, a Personality; focus cards go face down to the active area, Fate cards only; Chi plus the Focus Values decides it, doubled for Double Chi; `tokenchi`: a token's C counts in a duel). Version 15 (2026-11-04): the rulebook's own actions, by Scorpion Clan Coup pp. 48 and 50–51: `timing`, when each kind of action may be taken (Limited in the player's own Action Phase; Open in any Action Phase or a battle's Battle actions segment; Battle in that segment, with a unit in the battle; Reaction any time); the Imperial Favor's `lobby` (once a turn, a Limited and Political action: bow a Personality with Personal Honor; the highest Family Honor takes an unheld Favor, otherwise speakers and one gift each decide it) and `uses` (given up to draw a Fate card, restore a Dishonored Personality, send a unit home from a battle bowed, or prevent a loss of Family Honor). Version 16 (2026-11-05): card actions: `actions.fromhand` (which hand cards are played for their actions: Strategy, Ring) replaces `actions.words` and `actions.icons`; a card's actions now come from its own `play.actions` (below), else from its text read the same way, and their timing words are `timing`'s. Version 17 (2026-11-05): `terrain` (`keyword`: Terrain), a Terrain from the hand is played at the battle being fought, one there at a time, and its text takes effect once everyone has passed in the Battle Action Segment, before Force is compared (Imperial pp. 31, 38; Scorpion Clan Coup pp. 39, 48); it's discarded when the battle ends. Version 18 (2026-11-05): `phases[].auto`, a phase that goes on by itself once it has done what it does and nothing waits (an Event being resolved, a discard, a duel or lobbying): Straighten in every arc that has it, and the Events Phase from Clan Wars to Age of Enlightenment (provinces turn up one at a time and it stops at each Event, then goes on to Action). Samurai's and Emperor's Events Phase stays manual. A new game waits in its first phase; Back one step doesn't run a phase again. Version 19 (2026-11-05): `cardfields` gains `force` and `chi`, so a card action's target limit on Chi or Force ("Chi of less than five") is checked. Version 20 (2026-11-05): `cardfields` holds every numeric search field (the user: "All of the numeric search Fields should be in card fields"), adding `focus`, `honor`, `strength` and `startinghonor`; Focus Values discarded for a card's action (Kitsu Toju, Togashi Yoshi) counted as 0 before. A numeric field added to a game's search goes in `cardfields` too.
- New items are made with ootv-tools `structure/create.py <game>/play <file>`, which refuses if the item exists.

## What's in it

| Key | What |
|---|---|
| `zones` | the piles and areas (L5R's include the active area): who sees them, how they're laid out, which back a discard takes, what a tap does, which pile refills a province, where a destroyed province's cards go, which cards attach to the zone itself (`attach`, L5R's provinces: Regions and Fortifications) |
| `table.rows` | the order of the piles and provinces along a player's row |
| `table.arrange` | Arrange's rows of card types, nearest first (L5R: stronghold, sensei, wind, holdings, regions, celestials; then personalities; then the rest), and the types it stacks (Holding) |
| `placement`, `setup` | where each deck card starts (L5R: the stronghold and pre-game cards in play, in the corner; spots in card widths from the near right-hand corner) and the opening shuffles and deals |
| `cardfields`, `enter` | the card fields the rules read (L5R: type, keywords, title) and what happens as a card comes into play (L5R: Holdings enter bowed; Fortifications not, up to Lotus) |
| `cardstates`, `counters`, `cardcounters`, `customtokens`, `tokenforce` | bow and dishonor (upside down); Honor, Gold and Hand size; tokens on cards, by name (+1F, ...; any name typed), a token's F counted in battle |
| `tokens` | a token one player holds or nobody does (L5R: the Imperial Favor), how it's lobbied for (`lobby`) and what it's given up for (`uses`) |
| `timing` | when each kind of action may be taken (L5R: Limited, Open, Battle, Reaction) |
| `gold` | what produces Gold (`produce`), what a purchase is and what it costs (`buy`), the clan Personality rule (`clan`: Personal Honor when paid in full, else 2 less; not Shadowlands), and whether what's left after a payment is lost (`spendall`, before Ivory) |
| `handplay` | playing a card from the hand: which cards attach to the card they're played on (`attach`: L5R Item, Follower, Spell, Ancestor) and where the rest go while they resolve (`to`: the active area); the Gold Cost is paid as a purchase is |
| `actions` | card actions: `fromhand`, the hand cards played for their actions (Strategy, Ring). Each action is in its card's menu while its timing (`timing`) is open; using one picks its targets, pays its costs, makes the Force and Chi changes its text gives ("+2F for attacking until the end of the turn", "a 1F/2C bonus", tokens; a choice of bonuses or a condition is left to the players) and hands the action to the next player unless it was a Reaction |
| `terrain` | which hand cards are Terrains (`keyword`: Terrain): played at the battle being fought, one at a time, shown at that battle; its text takes effect when everyone has passed in the Battle Action Segment; discarded when the battle ends |
| `duel`, `tokenchi` | challenges and duels: who can duel (L5R: a Personality), where focus cards go face down (the active area) and which (Fate cards), the stat compared (Chi) and the card field added to it (Focus Value), who doubles it (Double Chi); a token's C counted in a duel |
| `firstplayer` | who goes first: the highest of a counter (L5R: Honor), a tie at random |
| `phases` | the turn: what each phase does as it starts (straighten, turn provinces up, draw), whose action it is, the hand limit, and the attack: its segments (Maneuvers: `assign`, the attacker then the defender; `infantry`, `cavalry` or `any`; Three Dynasties' Cavalry `onlywithunits`), the battle steps (Resolution: `resolve`), who may be assigned (`commit`: L5R, a unit led by an unbowed Personality; Cavalry when the Personality and all its Followers are) and the Honor a resolution gives (`honor`: 2 a card destroyed to the winner, 1 each on a tie) |
| `arcrules`, `rulesets` | what changes by arc or format, and the rule sets a player can pick at the start (see below) |

## L5R rules by arc

From the last complete rulebook of each arc (`ootv-play` `docs/l5r/turn-sequence`). The start screen asks for the
arc, which filters the deck lists, and the rules, which default to the arc's own; the legacy formats (Legacy,
BigDeck, Modern, Obsidian Hand) default to the latest arc's, Three Dynasties. Any rule set can be picked.

| Rules | Phases | Each battle | Starting hand |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clan Wars to Age of Enlightenment | Straighten, Events, Action, Attack, Dynasty, End | Battle actions, Resolution | 5 |
| Race for the Throne (Samurai) | the same | Combat, Resolution | 5 |
| Destroyer War (Celestial) | the same | Combat, Resolution | 6 |
| Age of Conquest (Emperor) | the same | Engage (attacker first), Combat, Resolution | 6 |
| Ivory, Twenty Festivals, Onyx / Shattered Empire | Action, Attack, Dynasty, End of turn | Engage, Combat, Resolution | 5 |
| Three Dynasties | the same, Infantry and Cavalry Maneuvers back | Engage, Combat, Resolution | 5 |

Everywhere: starting Honor from the stronghold, four provinces, a hand size of 8 (a counter players can change),
the defender acting first in battle unless noted.

Gold: before Ivory there's no pool (excess Gold is "taxed by the Emperor" and lost, Samurai and Emperor rules), so
there's no Gold counter. From Ivory on (Ivory, Twenty Festivals, Onyx, Shattered Empire, Three Dynasties and the
legacy formats) a Gold counter is the pool, emptied for both players as each phase ends.

Battles (all arcs): units assigned to the defender's provinces stand in a Battlefields pane, one column per province.
Resolution totals each army's printed Force (bowed cards add nothing, a bowed Personality makes its unit nothing, a
side with no units has none) and the province's Strength (the Stronghold's `strength`, plus attachments printing one),
which players adjust for card effects. The loser's army is destroyed, both on a tie with units on both sides; the
province falls when the attacking Force is more than the defence plus its Strength. End without resolving (beside
Resolve, 2026-11-05) ends a battle with no outcome, for a card that ends it or players agreeing: nothing destroyed,
no Honor, but the attackers still bow and go home and its Terrain is discarded (Emperor CR XIII.1.4). Attackers bow and go home after
each battle; defenders at the end of the attack, or when their province falls (Scorpion Clan Coup pp. 37–42, Ivory
CR XII.7–8, Emperor CR XIII). A destroyed province leaves the row.

## Card field `play`

`play` isn't in search documents and isn't searchable (since 2026-11-05): the table and the card editor read it with `/oracle-fetch`, and changes to it aren't written to the card's update log while the play table is in development (the user, 2026-11-05).

Rules a card brings to the table live on the card, not in `l5r/play` (the user, 2026-11-03, the same way `deckconstruction` does for deck checks). `{"attached": {...}}` changes the zone the card is attached to while it's there face up; only `autorefill` can be changed so far. Shinsei's Shrine (card 6992): `{"attached": {"autorefill": false}}`, "This province does not hold Dynasty cards (do not refill it)". `{"force": [{"when": "attacking", "add": 2}]}` adds to a card's Force in a battle while it's in the attacking army (`defending` for the defending one), for a card like Matsu Gohei ("+2F while attacking"). The province doesn't refill by itself; a player can still refill it by hand. Edit it in the card editor like any field. `{"actions": {"Imperial": [...]}}` holds the card's printed actions for a rule set (the user, 2026-11-05), one entry each: `name` (the label, e.g. "Battle", "Political Reaction"), `timing` (the timing words it may be used under: "Battle/Open" is `["Battle", "Open"]`, either may be used), `keywords` (the rest of the label: Repeatable, Elemental, ...), `cost` (`bow`: `true` bows the card, a word such as "Shugenja" bows the card it's attached to; `gold`: Gold paid), `targets` (`count`, `type` such as Personality or Follower, `whose`: `yours` or `theirs`, `bowed`/`unbowed`, `upto`, `text`) and `effect` (the printed words), and `effects`, what the table does (the user, 2026-11-05): a list of `{"add": {"force": 2}, "to": "target", "when": "attacking", "until": "turn"}` (`add`: a card's `force`/`chi`, or a player's `honor`/`gold` with `to` `you` or `opponent`; `to`: `target`, `self`, `you`, `opponent`, `all` with `all` `{type, side}`; `if`/`unless`: a match the card must or mustn't fit, for "3F to a Lion, or 2F to any other"; `when`: the change counts only while attacking or defending, its badge dimmed otherwise; `until`: `turn`, `battle`, `ever`), `{"token": "+2F/+2C Weapon", "to": "target"}`, or `{"set": {"dishonored": true}, "to": "target"}`; and battle effects (2026-11-05, Scorpion Clan Coup pp. 55–57): `{"ranged": 3, "to": "target"}` (a Ranged Attack: at an opposing Personality without Followers, or a Follower, in the battle being fought, destroyed when the strength is at least its Force; `{"stat": "chi", "of": "self"|"host", "plus": 2}` for "equal to her Chi" or "the Shugenja's Chi+2"; cards of one unit can combine theirs), `{"fear": 3, "to": "target"}` (bows the target unit's Followers with Force 3 or less, once a battle per card), `{"destroy": true, "to": "self"}`, and `add {"ranged": 2}` (Blazing Arrows). A bow is always the card's own `cost`, never the Ranged rule (the user, 2026-11-05: Shuriken of Serpents' cost is being destroyed instead). A target's `match` limits what it may be: `{"clan": ["Lion"]}`, `keywords`, `type`, or a current value `{"ph": {"min": 3}}`, `{"chi": {"max": 4}}`. The Ancestral Home of the Lion: target `{"match": {"clan": ["Lion"]}}`, effects `[{"add": {"force": 2}, "to": "target", "when": "attacking", "until": "turn"}]`; The Esteemed House of the Crane: `[{"add": {"honor": 2}, "to": "you"}]`. Without `effects` the table reads them from `effect`; "if", "when ..." and "for each" stay with the players. For Imperial they're read from the Clan Wars printing (Obsidian, Emerald or Imperial), not today's wording. A card without them has its text read the same way. Structured `effects` and `match` written 2026-11-05 on the 28 Imperial cards whose effects the table can do (each read by hand), then Ranged Attacks and Fear on 25 more (Goblin Mob's redirect stays with the players). Written 2026-11-05 for 186 Imperial Edition cards (ootv-tools `field-fixes/play-actions.py`; the printing's text went on 268 printings), with the targets and costs checked twice (a parser and the qwen3-vl model each read every action; disagreements ruled by hand). Search never reads `play`: since `l5r-os4` the index stores it unmapped. A game picks a card's change up when it's opened again or reset (both reload the card data, 2026-11-04); before that, a game kept the data from when its decks were loaded.

### Reactions (2026-11-05)

Nobody is asked whether they have a Reaction: that would hint at what's in their hand (the user, 2026-11-05). A player who wants one presses Reaction in the top bar, triple-clicks with a mouse (not on a deck or pile), or taps with three fingers, then picks the event from a list of what happened, back to their own last action (a Pass counts). The table shows the game as it stood just after that event, and the Reaction is played there; everything after it is taken back, not replayed, and the log says what was. Reactions answering an event form a stack (the user, 2026-11-05: "If reactions set up a chain, they are treated as a stack, that has to be fully resolved to get back to the original trigger to take the second reaction"): a new Reaction answers the top or a link further down, with nothing taken back, and answering one further down closes everything above it (no longer offered). So one player can react twice to the same event (Master of the Tea Ceremony, then Kakita Yinobu, on one loss), the second reading the game as it is then. The order is Scorpion Clan Coup p. 49's: after an action, Reactions in turn from the player on the actor's left until all pass. A cancelling Reaction has the effect `{"cancel": {"keywords": [...], "type": [...], "targeting": "you" | "personality", "token": true}}`: it cancels only an action with one of those keywords on its label or card, from a card of that type, aimed at you or your Personality, or (with `token`) the use of a token such as the Imperial Favor. The event's effects are undone and its log lines struck through; its costs stay paid and a card played from the hand still goes to its discard pile. `"to": "actor"` is the player whose action the Reaction answers: Investigation `[{"cancel": {"keywords": ["Ninja", "Kolat", "Assassin"], "targeting": "you"}}, {"add": {"honor": -5}, "to": "actor"}]`. An event's kind ("after a Personality is destroyed") is checked where a play code says so (`after`, Honor actions below). The `l5r/fields` description of `play` has `cancel` and `actor` since version 19.

### Honor actions (2026-11-05)

The Honor group of card actions (the user: "do the honor cards"). Most answer something that just happened, so a play code can say when it may be used, checked before anything is paid: `{"after": ...}` what the event reacted to did, or the battle just resolved (`battledestroy`: you destroyed an army or a Province as the Attacker or Defender, Brilliant Victory and Famous Poet; `battlewin`: you won and destroyed the other army, Mercy; `provinceheld`: your Province was attacked and stood, Ring of Earth; `honorloss`: you lost Family Honor in it, Master of the Tea Ceremony and Kakita Yinobu; `restored`: this card was restored to Honorable status in it, Toturi; `death`: a Personality of yours was destroyed in it, Martyr; `seppuku`: the target was dishonored in it, or you lost Honor, Honorable Seppuku); `{"battle": "outforced" | "outforcing"}` (the other army's Force higher, or lower with units: Legendary Victory, Refuse Advantage); `{"once": "battle" | "ever"}`. What they do: `{"take": "favor"}` (Kakita Yoshi), `{"award": {"double": true} | {"plus": 8}}` (if you win the battle being fought), `{"even": true}` (your army's Force lowered to the other's for the battle), `{"refund": "toone" | "all" | "discard"}` (Honor lost in the event given back), `{"spare": {"each": 3}}` (Mercy: the army comes back as it stood, 3 Honor a card instead of the award), `{"intoplay": true}` (a Ring comes into play). `"to": "highest"` is the player or players with the highest Family Honor (Breach of Etiquette), `"to": "owner"` the target's player (Shame); an amount can be `{"of": "target", "field": "ph"}` or `{"of": "discard"}`; a target can need `"state": "dishonored"`; a cost can discard hand cards, `{"discard": {"count": 1}}` (picked after the targets). Left to the players: Shame's "only one each time a Personality is dishonored", Ring of Earth's "at any point in the battle". The `l5r/fields` description of `play` has these since version 20.

### Into play, tokens, take control (2026-11-06)

The next group of card actions (the user: "Can you do the next group?"). `{"make": {"title", "type", "force", "chi", "ph", "keywords"}}` puts a token card into play, attached to `"to": "self"` or the target, or on its own (Matsu Imura's animal, Summon Swamp Spirits, Summon Undead Champion); the table shows it as a text card with its name, numbers and traits, and it leaves the game when it leaves play. `{"control": {...}}` takes control of the target and what is attached to it, into the player's play area: `"attach": "self"` attaches it to the card (Matsu Imura, The Demon Bride of Fu Leng), `"drop"` destroys the attached cards that fit first (Kolat Master: Followers with required Honor above zero), `"while": "source"` holds it only while the card stays in play (Blackmail). A card under another player's control is tagged with its owner's name and goes to its owner's piles when it leaves play. `{"bring": {"attach": "self" | "host" | n, "face": "down", "pay": "cost"}}` brings the target into play under the player's control, picked from a hand or discard pile with the target's `"from": "hand" | "discard" | "discards"` (Necromancer, Animate the Dead, Cloak of Night face down, Feign Death, Scribe onto its Shugenja paying the Spell's Gold Cost); clicking a discard pile then offers its cards that fit. `{"gain": {"keywords", "actions"}}` adds traits and actions while the card stays in play (Animate the Dead: Shadowlands and "Battle: Fear 3"; Kolat Master: Kolat). `{"copy": true}` brings the card into play as a duplicate of the target with its permanent changes (The Egg of P'an Ku; it shows the copied card's picture, tagged with its own name). `{"rally": true}` moves the Followers of the player's Personality that died in the event onto the target (Rally Troops). `"after": "gone"`: the target left play in the event reacted to. An action's `"ask": {"max", "text"}` asks the player for a number, used by a token's `"count": {"of": "x"}` (Togashi Mitsu's fire tokens, at most 5). Left to the players: Debt of Honor, Mitsu's straightening and bowing, Blackmail's and Cloak of Night's reveals, the Egg's Unique, Scribe's once per spell. Matsu Imura takes a Follower with the trait Creature (the user, 2026-11-06: later sets have some). Written on 14 Imperial cards; the `l5r/fields` description of `play` has these since version 21.

### Destroy (2026-11-06)

The Destroy group (the user: "start on destroy"). Most use `{"destroy": true}` on what is there already: the target (Alhundro Cornejo, Explosives, Kolat Assassin, Remorseful Seppuku on a Dishonored Samurai, Touch of Death on a bowed Personality with `"state": "bowed"`), `"to": "self"` for "destroy this spell" (Fist of Osano-Wo, Touch of Death, Heart of the Inferno), `"to": "all"` with `"if"` for everything that fits in the battle (Flight of Dragons: the trait Dragon, either side, not Dragon Clan; Heart of the Inferno: the opposing army's Personalities and Followers with Force 2 or less). A target's `match` can name keywords (a Retainer, a Holding's trait: Kolat Assassin, Matsu Hiroru) or a `title` (Terrible Standard of Fu Leng: Hida Sukune, once a game, +4F). New: a target's `"army": "opposing"` (in the opposing army at the battle being fought, with this card's unit there: Hida Yakamo) and `"attacked": true` (attached to the Province this card's unit is attacking: Battering Ram Crew, Fire Breather), and `{"destroy": {"attached": {"type": "Follower"}}}`, which destroys the target's attached cards that fit, not the target (Break Morale). Written on 14 Imperial cards; the `l5r/fields` description of `play` has these since version 22.

### Force, Chi, Province Strength (2026-11-06)

The next group of card actions. `"to": "inplay"` gives an effect to every face-up card in play, either side, and `"to": "yours"` to the player's, each narrowed by `"if"` and `"unless"` (Naga Shugenja: +1F to every Naga card; Evil Portents: Shadowlands Personalities and Followers +1F/+1C, every other Personality −1F/−1C); a match's `"clan": "yours"` is the player's clan. `{"align": true}` aligns the cards with the player's clan, losing their own (Oath of Fealty, with +1F/+1C `"until": "ever"`; tagged with the clan). `{"swap": true}` switches the target's Force and Chi until the end of the turn (Energy Transference; its `"iftarget": "theirs"` destroys the spell only when the target is another player's). `{"double": true}` doubles a weapon's Force and Chi bonuses for the rest of the game, once a weapon (Immortal Steel; tagged "Bonuses ×2"). `{"nodestroy": true}` marks a card for the turn: an army containing it destroys neither the opposing army nor the Province at Resolution (Mists of Illusion; tagged "Can't destroy"). `{"strength": n or {"of": "discard"}, "to": "target" | "provinces"}` changes a Province's Strength, until the end of the turn unless `"until": "ever"`, which stops at zero (Castle of Water and The War Fortress of the Crab +3 on a Province, picked by tapping it or its card; Isawa Tadaka on all the player's Provinces by the Focus Value discarded; Alhundro Cornejo −1 for good); a changed Province shows a "Str +3" badge and the battle totals count it. An action's `"anywhere": true` can be used in a battle the player has no units in. `{"battle": "defending" | "attacking" | "unopposed"}` and `{"once": "turn"}` say when an action may be used (Earth Dragon once a turn while defending; Tsuburu no Oni attacking with no defending units, taken as a Reaction at the end of the Battle actions segment). A token's `"hold": "stay"` keeps it only while the card stays bowed in play (Bayushi Kachiko's seduction token: the Straighten Phase leaves her bowed and adds a token; straightening her by hand, or her leaving play, takes them all off). Gaijin Mercenaries needed nothing new. Written on 14 Imperial cards; the `l5r/fields` description of `play` has these since version 23.

### Cards: draw, discard, look, search (2026-11-06)

`{"draw": n}` draws from the player's Fate deck (Look into the Void, Isawa Kaede). `{"discard": true}` on a Province target discards its card, and the Province refills as usual (Kaede on the player's own, Winds of Change on anyone's). `{"discard": {"random": n}, "to": "opponent"}` discards hand cards at random (Kuni Yori), `{"discard": "hand"}` all of them (Void Dragon). `{"look": "hand" | "provinces", "to": "opponent"}` lets the player see the other player's hand or face-down Province cards (Ninja Spy); the log names them to the looker alone and tells the table a look happened. `{"look": {"count": n | {"of": "x"} | {"of": "discard"}}}` lets the player look at that many face-down cards of anyone's on the table (Oracle of Wind's X Gold, Isawa Uona's Focus Value): another player's face-down card then has "Look (N left)" in its menu. A target's `"facedown": true` is a face-down card on the table that isn't in play; `{"turn": true}` turns it up (Call Upon the Wind). `{"test": "honor"}` makes the target's controller discard the top Fate card: a Focus Value above its Personal Honor dishonors it, then destroys it (Test of Honor). `{"search": "fate", "draws": 1}` opens a search of the player's Fate deck from which only that many cards may leave, to the hand, and the deck is shuffled when the search ends (Walking the Way). `"after": "provincefell"`: the battle just resolved destroyed a Province with this card in the attacking army (Void Dragon); `"after": "enddraw"`: the End Phase just drew into the player's empty hand (Ring of the Void draws four more). An action's `"inplay": true` can't be used to play the card from the hand. Written on 12 Imperial cards (13 actions); the `l5r/fields` description of `play` has these since version 24.

### Move, remove from battle, send home, assign (2026-11-06)

`{"home": "bowed" | "unbowed" | true}` sends the target's unit home from its battle: bowed (Block Supply Lines, Retreat, Miya Yoto), unbowed (Kakita Yuri). `{"move": "battle"}` assigns an unassigned unit of the player's to the battle being fought (Oracle of Water); `{"move": n}` moves the target's unit to the Defender's Province picked as target n (Isawa Tomo, Superior Tactics); `{"move": "switch"}` has two units trade places, assigned or at home, without moving (Way of Deception). `{"attach": n}` moves the target, attached to a Personality, to the Personality picked as target n, under that Personality's controller (Iuchi Karasu's Follower, Ninja Thief's Item). `{"endbattle": true}` sends every unit at the battle home bowed and ends it without resolution (Torrential Rain). `{"destroy": "terrain"}` destroys the Terrain at the battle being fought (Superior Tactics, whose either/or is two actions, as its Soul of the Empire printing has it). `{"ambush": true}`: target 0 (the player's unit) and target 1 (another player's) fight a battle of their own in the Action Phase, on a battlefield made for it (an empty zone, no Province card, Region, Fortification or Stronghold Strength; Strength 0) that is removed when the battle ends; no other unit joins it or moves from it, and the next player acts after it (Ambush). `"ahead": true` makes an effect happen only while the player's army has the higher Force, and `"to": "leader"` aims it at the Personality leading the target's unit (Retreat's dishonor, seen before the unit leaves). Gates `{"battle": "defender"}` (only as the Defender: Kakita Yuri) and `{"battle": "outside"}` (only at a battle where the player has no units and isn't the Defender: Miya Yoto). Targets `"whose": "attacking" | "defending"` (a unit on that side of the battle being fought), `"whose": "defender"` on a Province, `"at": "battle" | "assigned" | "home"`; `"unbowed"` on a unit is its Personality's. A Gold cost `{"of": "unit"}` is the target unit's Force, its unbowed cards counted (Kakita Yuri). A card rule outside the actions, `"assign": {"Imperial": "defender"}`: while the card is in play, the defender assigns first in each Maneuvers segment of its owner's turn (Ring of Water). Written on 13 Imperial cards (14 actions); the `l5r/fields` description of `play` has these since version 25.

### Copy, rule changes (2026-11-06)

`{"perform": {...}}` produces another card's action now, as this card's: the player picks a card in play and which of its actions, then that action's targets. `"action"` limits which actions (`{"keywords": "Elemental"}`), `"from"` which cards (`{"keywords": "Oni"}`); its timing must be open; its costs aren't paid (this action's are the price) unless `"pay": true`, and its "destroy this card" is left out; `"once": "discarded"` copies a spell that destroys itself only once (Naka Kuro). `{"learn": {...}}` gives the picked action, copied as it is, until the end of the turn, used with its own costs, so a bow cost makes it once a turn and an action without one can be used again (the user: "the action is copied perfectly"): the target's own action with `"source": "target"` (Water Dragon, Ninja Shapeshifter's "one ability"), to the target with `"to": "target"` (Reflective Pool) or to the card this one is on with `"to": "host"` (Mask of the Oni). `{"mimic": "force" | "chi" | "ph"}` takes the target's value for the turn (Ninja Shapeshifter; its four choices are four actions sharing one `{"once": "turn", "key": "copy"}`). `{"redirect": true}`, a Reaction to a spell being used: its effects are undone (its costs stay paid) and done again at targets the player picks anew, as its caster's (Ring of Air, once a turn). `{"first": "attacker"}` with the gate `{"battle": "start"}` (before anyone has acted in the battle): the Attacker acts first (Sneak Attack). `{"counter": true}` with `"after": "attackend"` (an opponent's attack in which you were the Defender has just ended): an extra Attack Phase in which you attack the former Attacker; the phase it was played in goes on after (Counterattack). `{"unbow": "battle"}` with `"after": "fought"`: your cards the battle just resolved bowed straighten (Rallying Cry). The Rings' conditions: `{"hand": "empty"}` (no other card in your hand: Ring of the Void), `{"produced": 3}` (three spells or Shugenja abilities used this turn: Ring of Air), `"after": "terrains"` (in the battle just resolved you destroyed a Terrain, played a new one, and destroyed an army or a Province: Ring of Water). `"gain"` also takes `"until": "turn"`, `"uses"` and `"to": "self" | "host"`. Water Dragon's action has the gate `{"phase": "Events"}`, and the card rule `"waits": {"Imperial": {"phase": "Events", "action": 0}}` keeps the Events Phase from going on by itself while the Dragon is in play and hasn't copied this turn (the user: "no reason the water dragon couldn't suspend the auto-proceed"): its owner gets Copy an ability… and, while last turn's Shugenja is still in play, Keep (the same copy); the phase button goes on without it. Written on 11 Imperial cards (12 actions); the `l5r/fields` description of `play` has these since version 26, Water Dragon's `waits` since 27.

### Bow, straighten, dishonor; Gold and costs; Cavalry; Terrain swaps (2026-11-06)

The last four groups of card actions (the user: "Do all 4 groups"), 23 actions on 22 Imperial cards. `{"set": {"bowed": true}}` bows a target and `false` straightens one; `"target": n` picks which target slot, for an action that also bows one of your own cards (Sake Works, Yasuki Taka); `"to": "host"` is the card it's on (Spirit Guide straightens its Shugenja). `{"nostraighten": ...}` keeps the target bowed: `"bowed"` while this card stays bowed, which it may (Kolat Servant; straighten it by hand to let go), `"there"` while this card is on the table (Shady Dealings, with `"to": "stronghold"`), `"next"` at its controller's next Straighten (Summon Faeries, only with a Phoenix caster: `"by": {"clan": ["Phoenix"]}`). Ikoma Ujiaki's gate `"after": "killed"` with `"killed": {"clan": ["Lion"], "type": "Personality"}` needs one of your Lion Personalities destroyed in the battle just resolved; his target's `"army": "opposed"` is one that was in the army facing yours.

`{"gold": n}` or `{"gold": {"of": "discard"}}` produces Gold for you (Shady Dealings' 5; Asahina Tamako: the discarded card's Focus Value). `{"goldplus": 1, "match": {"type": "Holding"}}` makes every Holding that produces Gold produce one more this turn (Bountiful Harvest). `{"free": match}` makes the next card of yours that fits, with a number Gold Cost, come into play free this turn (School of Wizardry: a Shugenja Personality, `"unless"` Naga or Nonhuman; Alhundro Cornejo: the Strategy Explosives); the table doesn't ask about paying for it. Shady Dealings stays in the active area until it's repaid: its second action (Limited, 5 Gold) destroys it, and the Stronghold may straighten again.

`{"trait": "Cavalry", "until": "turn"}` gives the target, or with `"to": "unit"` every card in the target's unit, the Cavalry keyword for the turn (Iuchi Takaai, Wind Born Speed, Wings of Fire, The Provincial Estate of the Unicorn; Shinjo Yasamura's target must be in his unit, `"same": "unit"`, once a turn). Cavalry Maneuvers counts it, and a printed `<b>Cavalry</b>` now counts too.

`{"terrain": "target"}` plays the target Terrain at the battle being fought, destroying the Terrain there (Go Master); `"keep": true` means it can't be destroyed or replaced (Oracle of Earth). `{"store": true}` puts a Terrain from the hand face down under The Armor of Sun Tao (the other player isn't told which, target `"hidden": true`); its Battle action plays one from there (target `"from": "under"`; on a phone, tap the Armor while picking to choose from a list).
