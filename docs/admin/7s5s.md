# 7th Sea: Five Sails (table `7s5s`)

Last updated: 2026-10-28

**7th Sea: City of Five Sails**, Pine Box Entertainment's card game (2023–), shown as "7th Sea: Five
Sails": the Base Game (2023), Tooth & Claw (2024), Fate & Fortune (2025), Blood & Salt (2026) and the
City deck updates of 2024 and 2026. Cards print "© <year> Moon Design". Loaded 2026-10-28 (ootv-claude
`projects/newgame-7s5s.md`). Until its release it's a preview game: in the menu on
preview.oracleofthevoid.com only.

**Not the 1999 7th Sea CCG** (table `7thsea`). The two share a setting and many names (nations, Sea
Dogs, Eisenfaust): they are separate games, never matched.

## Where the data came from

- **Cards**: Five Sails DB (https://sailsdb.onrender.com/, a fan database; its open API), 423 records,
  converted by ootv-games `game-7s5s/scripts/convert.py`. **423 cards, one printing each** (no reprints
  yet).
- **Checked against the cards**: Five Sails DB calls itself an alpha, and some of its text is a
  shorthand paraphrase rather than the printed wording (most of Base Game 7S5S-98 to 7S5S-115).
  Every card image was read by a vision model, and every card any read flagged was checked by eye
  (136 cards). 84 cards carry corrections, kept in ootv-games `game-7s5s/fixes.json` so a refresh
  keeps them: 40 texts, 15 artists, names and subtitles, about 30 stats, and two records
  (7S5S-188/190) whose data was half swapped.
- **Images**: Five Sails DB's 750×1050 renders, with rounded corners cut.

## Cards and printings

- `title` is "Name: Subtitle" ("Cesca del Rosso: Donna Sinistra"); `subtitle` holds the italic line
  alone, which tells apart the 13 names that have two cards (a Leader and an Approach version).
- Card fields, named as in the rulebook's card anatomy: `type` (Character, Risk, Attachment, Scheme,
  Event, Location), `deck` (Leader, Approach, Faction, City), `faction` (Castille, Eisen, Montaigne,
  Ussura, Vodacce, Neutral, City), `traits`, `cost` (wealth cost), `resolve`, `combat`, `finesse`,
  `influence`, `crewcap` and `panache` (Leaders; on schemes `panache` is the modifier), `initiative`
  (schemes), `riposte`, `parry`, `thrust` (Faction-deck cards), `text`. Stats keep their printed signs
  and dashes ("+1", "-").
- In `text`, icons are tokens the game config draws: `[riposte]` `[parry]` `[thrust]` `[combat]`
  `[finesse]` `[influence]` `[resolve]` `[panache]`, the locations `[docks]` `[forums]` `[bazaar]`
  `[olesinn]` `[gardens]`; a scheme's separator line is `<hr>`.
- Printing fields: `set`, `number`, `code` (as printed; City deck cards print only "CU2024"/"CU2026"),
  `citydecknumber` (the number in a City deck card's coin), `artist` (as printed; `artistnotes` where
  the credit was corrected), `flavor`, `credit` (lines like "Card Designed by ..."), `notes`
  (misprints, e.g. Henri Michelet printed as 7S5S-67). Internal: `sailsdbid`. No rarity (fixed
  expansions).

## Legality

None: the floor rules make every released card legal.

## Refreshing

Pine Box still releases cards. ootv-games `game-7s5s/scripts/refresh.sh` pulls Five Sails DB and
reports what changed (`convert/refresh-<date>/changes.md`); new cards need the scan checks there before
they go in (upload-set.py for new cards, the bulk recipe for changed ones). `ADD-GAME.md` and
`GO-LIVE.md` there record how the game was added.
