# Public lists

**Public lists** (the globe in the menu bar) shows the decks and lists other players have
shared, including tournament-winning decks and the pre-constructed starter decks.

## Finding decks {#search}

- The first box searches deck names, notes and players' names. The **card** box finds decks
  with a card in them, by its title. When two cards share a title, type the card's number
  instead (`#1234`, the number at the end of the card page's link) to find exactly that one.
- The pull-downs narrow by type, and in L5R by clan, stronghold and format.
- **Pre-constructed** shows only starter and learn-to-play decks. **Tournament results** shows
  only decks with a tournament result.
- The last pull-down sorts them. **Reset** clears everything.

More load as you scroll down. An L5R deck shows its dynasty/fate cards and total ("40/40 (83 total)"),
with a red **Illegal** when it breaks the deck construction rules (as its owner last saw it).

## Your own lists {#mine}

Logged in, the first pull-down switches between **Public lists** and **My lists**. **My lists**
searches your own lists, private ones too, with the same boxes and pull-downs, and the
**Decks**, **Formats** and **Most played** views. It shows every kind of list (pick one in the
type pull-down); each says whether it's public or private. Nobody else sees your private lists
here. A list you just saved shows up within a few seconds.

On a card's page, the folder next to the globe opens your lists with that card, with how many
of them hold it. It goes by the card itself, not its title, so two cards with the same name stay
apart.

## Decks, People, Formats, Most played {#views}

The buttons on the right of the menu bar group the results:

- **Decks**: one card per deck.
- **People**: one card per player, with how many of each kind of list they've shared. Click to
  see their lists.
- **Formats**: how many decks there are for each format. Click one to see them. Decks set to
  **Special** ([no limits](lists.md#stats)) have a row of their own.
- **Most played**: the cards the decks play most ([below](#most-played)).

"by *name*" on a deck shows that player's other lists.

## Most played cards {#most-played}

**Most played** (the bar chart button) ranks the cards in the public decks. It counts the decks
that the filters find, so pick a format, a clan or **Tournament results** to see what those decks
play. Each card shows:

- **In N decks**: how many of the decks have at least one copy, and what share of them that is.
  Click it to see those decks.
- **copies**: the copies over all of them, so a card played three to a deck counts three times.

**Most decks** / **Most copies** picks which count comes first, and **Any card type** narrows it to
one type of card. Click a card to open it. Only decks count, not collections or have and want
lists. Old tournament archives make up a large share of the L5R decks, so their eras weigh more.

On a card's page, and beside each card in the detailed search results, the number next to the globe
is how many public decks play it. Click it to see them.

## Opening a deck {#open}

Click a deck to open it. Someone else's deck is read only. Logged in, **Copy to my lists**
makes your own private copy to change; the copy notes where it came from. The deck's menu has
its downloads, the same as your own lists have ([Downloads](lists.md#downloads)).

To share one of your own lists, see [Sharing](lists.md#sharing).
