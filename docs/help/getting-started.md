# Getting started

Oracle of the Void is a card database and deck builder for several collectible card games.
You can use it without an account. Log in (free) to build [decks and lists](lists.md).

## Pick a game {#game}

The game's name in the menu bar opens the list of games. Each game has its own cards, search
form and rules. The site remembers the last game you used.

## Find cards {#find}

Type in the search form on the left (on a phone, under **Edit search**) and press **Search**.
The [Searching](searching.md) page explains every box and the search language of the All box.

On Help, the Bookshelf and Public lists the form starts collapsed. The small arrow at the left edge of the page opens it.

A small **?** next to a control shows its help when you hover over it (on a phone, press and
hold it). Click or tap it to open the whole help page there.

## The menu bar {#menu}

The icons in the middle switch between the site's views. Hover over one (or press and hold it
on a phone) to see its name.

- **Search results**: the cards your last search found. Click it again to show or hide the form.
- **Card details**: the card you opened, with every printing, its rulings and its legality
  ([The card page](cards.md)).
- **List/Deck directory** and **Deck/List details**: your decks and lists, logged in
  ([Decks and lists](lists.md)).
- **Public lists**: decks and lists other people have shared ([Public lists](public-lists.md)).
- **Bookshelf**: the rulebooks and newsletters, to read and search ([Bookshelf](rules.md)).
- **Help**: these pages.

The buttons on the right change how results look and how they're sorted. Settings is in the
menu under your name, or the first menu when logged out ([Account and settings](settings.md)).

## Pulldowns {#pulldowns}

Click or tap a pulldown and start typing to narrow its choices, then press Enter or pick one. Short ones with only two choices are ordinary pulldowns.

## Links and sharing {#links}

The address bar always shows what's on screen: a search, a card, a list, a rulebook page or a
help page. Bookmark it or send it to someone, and it opens in the same place. Back and Forward
work as usual.

## Getting help {#community}

Ask on Discord (where our bot can look up cards: see [Discord bot](discord-bot.md)), report
problems or ask for features on GitHub, and watch the how-to videos on YouTube (the links at
the top of this page).
