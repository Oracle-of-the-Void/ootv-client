# The card page

Click a card in the search results, a list or a link to open it in **Card details**. The
address bar then points at that card (and that printing), so the link can be shared.

## Moving around {#moving}

- The arrows beside the card's name go to the previous and next search result. So do the
  arrow keys, and a sideways swipe on a phone (Settings → Gestures can turn the swipe off).
- **X** goes back to where you were. Escape does the same.
- The globe beside the name shows the [public lists](public-lists.md) that have this card.

## Versions {#versions}

A card can have several printings: the same card in different sets, sometimes with different
art, flavor text or wording. The **Versions** buttons list them as *(number) set / rarity*. Click
one to show its image and details. The one marked **\*** is the main version, whose wording is
the card's current text.

Each printing's own set, rarity, flavor text, artist and so on are listed below the card's
details.

## Printed text or current text {#text-version}

When the page shows one printing, its text can be either the wording printed on that card or
the card's current wording (its latest version, with errata). The **Printed text** /
**MRP text** button at the top of the text switches between them for the rest of your visit.
Settings → Display sets which one new visits start with.

## Click to search {#click-search}

Many values on the card are links: a clan, a type, a keyword, an artist. Clicking one searches
for every card with it, keeping the rest of your search form.

An artist works a little differently: clicking the name opens a small box with **Search cards by**
that artist, then the artist's own pages (portfolio, gallery, social media) where we know them.
Links marked "archived copy" go to an old saved copy of a site that is gone.

## Rulings {#rulings}

**Rulings** collects official rulings, FAQ answers and errata for the card. Many end in a
link to the rulebook or FAQ page they come from: hover over the link to see that page, marked
where the ruling is (on a phone, press and hold it), and click it to open the page.
**Open in the Bookshelf** takes you to that place in the [Bookshelf](rules.md).

## Rules Q&A {#rules-qa}

The **answers from the rules team** button lists answers the official rules team gave about
this card, mostly from the old mailing-list rules digests. Click one to read it where it was
posted, with the question it answered.

## Traits and keywords in the rulebooks {#traits}

Keywords and traits that the rulebooks define (Cavalry, Kharmic, Experienced and so on) are
linked to those rules. A small book after a keyword, or a dotted line under a word in the text,
means there's a rule for it: hover (or press and hold) to read the rule, click to open the
rulebook at that page. Where several rulebooks define it, you choose which one; the rulebook
that matches the printing shown comes first.

## Adding the card to a list {#lists}

With a list active (see [Decks and lists](lists.md#active)), the bottom of the card has
+ and − buttons for it: one row for the card in general, and one for each printing, so you can
keep track of exactly which versions you have.

## Latest updates {#updates}

**Latest updates** at the bottom shows what was changed on this card and when.
