# Rules

**Rules** (the open book in the menu bar) is a shelf of the game's rulebooks, FAQs and
rules sheets, to read and search online.

## The shelf {#shelf}

The books are in order of release. The bar above them narrows the shelf by type (complete
rules, learn to play, addendum, special formats, other ways to play, floor rules, rulings), by
tag (such as **Base set** or **Last complete rules in arc**), and by arc. The site remembers
your choice. A book opens at the page you last read.

## Reading {#reading}

- Turn pages with the arrows at either side, a click or tap on the left or right of the page,
  a swipe, the arrow keys or the mouse wheel. **Home** and **End** go to the first and last page.
- Type a page number in the page box, or drag the slider to flip through.
- **One page / two pages** shows pages side by side on a wide screen.
- **Full screen** (or **F**) uses the whole screen.

## Zooming {#zoom}

Zoom with **+** and **−** (the buttons or the keys), Ctrl and the mouse wheel, or by pinching. A
double-click or double-tap zooms in there and back out. Drag a zoomed page around. **Fit the
page** (or **0**) goes back to the whole page.

## Searching a book {#search}

**Search this book** finds words in it and lists the pages they're on, marking them on the page.
The browser's own find (Ctrl+F) works on the pages shown too.

**Contents** lists the book's chapters, and **Pages** shows small pictures of every page.

## Copying text and linking {#copy}

- **Select text** (or **T**) lets you select words on the page to copy, like any web page.
- **Copy the text of this page** (or **C**) copies the whole page.
- **Copy a link** copies a link to the page shown, or, with text selected, to that text: the
  link opens the book there, with the text marked.

## From a card {#from-cards}

Rulings and traits on a card page link to the rulebook page they come from
([how](cards.md#rulings)); **Open in Rules** opens that page here.
