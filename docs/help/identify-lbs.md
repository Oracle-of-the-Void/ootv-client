# Identifying LBS cards

Which set a Legend of the Burning Sands card was printed in, read off the card itself. LBS had four
sets (1998–1999), in this order: Shadow of the Tyrant, Secrets and Lies, Black Hand, Black Heart and
The Awakening. Cards were reprinted from set to set and in the starter decks, so the same card can
come from several sets. The only thing that tells them apart is a tiny mark printed just before
"Illus" in the artist line, in the lower right corner.

## The set marks {#marks}

| Set | Mark | Look |
|---|---|---|
| Shadow of the Tyrant | ![Shadow of the Tyrant](res/help/identify-lbs/action-st.jpg) | no mark: the line starts with "Illus" |
| Secrets and Lies | ![Secrets and Lies](res/help/identify-lbs/action-sl.jpg) | a small cross |
| Black Hand, Black Heart | ![Black Hand, Black Heart](res/help/identify-lbs/action-bh.jpg) | four dots in a diamond |
| The Awakening | ![The Awakening](res/help/identify-lbs/action-aw.jpg) | a crescent, and the date reads ©1999 |
| Promotional cards | ![Promo](res/help/identify-lbs/promo.jpg) | a star |

A card has the same mark whatever its type or rarity. A card reprinted in a later set or starter
deck has that set's mark, not the mark of the set it first came out in.

## Holdings {#holdings}

On holdings the artist line runs up the right-hand edge, over the stones of the border, and the mark
sits at its very bottom, where the stone ends. That makes it hard to see, so look at the colour of
the type first:

| Set | Holding | Look |
|---|---|---|
| Shadow of the Tyrant | ![Shadow of the Tyrant holding](res/help/identify-lbs/holding-sott.jpg) | **white** type, no mark |
| Secrets and Lies | ![Secrets and Lies holding](res/help/identify-lbs/holding-sal.jpg) | **black** type, with black behind part of the line; the cross is cut off by the edge of the stone, so it looks like a little hook below "Illus" |
| Black Hand, Black Heart | ![Black Hand, Black Heart holding](res/help/identify-lbs/holding-bhbh.jpg) | **black** type; the dots sit half off the bottom of the stone, a small, barely visible nub below "Illus" |
| The Awakening | ![The Awakening holding](res/help/identify-lbs/holding-ta.jpg) | ©1999, and the crescent below "Illus" |

So on a holding: white type is Shadow of the Tyrant; black type with a hook is Secrets and Lies;
black type with a nub is Black Hand, Black Heart; 1999 is The Awakening.

## Look-alikes {#look-alikes}

- **No mark is not always Shadow of the Tyrant.** The mark is very small: on a worn card or a poor
  scan it can vanish. Check the date (The Awakening says 1999) and, on holdings, the colour of the
  type before calling a card Shadow of the Tyrant.
- **Cross and four dots** are about the same size and both pale. Under a magnifier the cross has
  arms with flared ends; the dots are four separate points.
- **Secrets and Lies and Black Hand, Black Heart holdings** both print black type; only the hook or
  the nub at the bottom of the stone tells them apart. Look at the card in good light.
