# Your account and settings

You can search, read cards and rules, and look at public lists without an account. Log in
(free; **Log In/Register** at the top right) to build decks and lists and to keep your settings
on every device.

**Settings** is in the menu under your name (logged out, in the first menu). Logged out, the
settings are kept in that browser only, and **Clear Cache** deletes them.

## Account {#account}

- **Name**: shown at the top of the page. Phones show only the first name, so keep it short.
- **Display name**: shown to others on the lists you make public. Leave it empty to show your
  initials.
- **Emails**: signing in with any of them reaches this account. A new address gets a code to
  confirm it.
- **Patreon**: our [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/oracleofthevoid) supporters get more
  [photo searches](searching.md#photo). A Patreon account whose email is one of your emails is
  tied to your account by itself. If it uses another email, **Link with Patreon** signs in to
  Patreon to tie it instead. **Unlink** unties it, and the email match won't tie it again.
- **Favorite game**: the game the site opens with in a new browser.

## Display {#display}

- **Card text**: whether cards start with their printed wording or their current wording
  ([more](cards.md#text-version)).
- **Saved views**: the layout and sort each part of the site opens with for this game. Set
  things up how you like them, then **Save**. Phones keep their own set.

## Printing {#printing}

The starting options for PDF printing (layout, paper, card size, the grid of cards, margins and
so on). The print dialog starts from them each time. See [Downloads](lists.md#downloads).

## Gestures {#gestures}

Swipes and long-presses, each one on or off: swiping cards to change their count, dragging in
the list directory, long-pressing a button to see its name, swiping between cards. Turn one off
to get the browser's usual behavior back. For card swipes, choose what each direction does (add,
remove, set the count, remove all), separately for search results and for lists.

## Searches {#searches}

Your default search and saved searches for this game, made with the search form's
**Save Search** and **Save as Default** ([more](searching.md#form)). Delete them here.

## Clear Cache {#clear-cache}

The site keeps cards and searches in your browser so they open quickly. If something looks out
of date, **Clear Cache** in the first menu throws that away and loads everything fresh.
