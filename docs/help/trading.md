# Trading and messages

Keep a list of the cards you want and a list of the ones you'd trade away, and the site finds the
players you can trade with. **Trades** (the two arrows in the menu bar) shows them; **Messages**
(the envelope) is where you talk it over.

## How a trade works {#how}

1. **Make your lists.** A Want list of the cards you're after, a Have list of the ones you'd part
   with, each set to **Open to trade** ([below](#open)).
2. **Find a trader.** **Trades** lists the players whose lists fit yours ([your matches](#matches)),
   or look up [who has a card](#card) from its page.
3. **Talk it over.** **Message** on a trader starts a conversation that names the cards
   ([messages](#messages)). Agree between you which cards go each way, their condition, and how
   you'll send them.
4. **Swap the cards.** The site doesn't take part in the trade itself: no shipping, no payment, no
   guarantee. You and the other player arrange that.
5. **Update your lists** afterwards ([after a trade](#after)), so you stop matching cards you no longer
   have or need.

Trading is per game: **Trades** shows the game you're in, so switch games to see the others.

## Opening a list to trade {#open}

1. Make a list of the cards you're after and set its type to **Want**, and one of the cards you'd
   trade away, type **Have**. Collections don't count: make a Have list of the cards you'd part with.
2. In the list view, set each one to **Open to trade**. That also makes the list Public.

You need a display name first (Settings), so traders know who you are, and **Accept messages**
switched on. Set a list back to **Not for trade** (or Private) to stop trading it.

A card on a Want list matches any printing of it, unless you picked a printing.

Foil counts too. Mark each entry with the foil toggle in the list view: a foil want matches only foil
haves, a regular want only regular ones, and **Foil or not** (Want lists) matches either. A printing
that was only made in foil is always foil.

## Your matches {#matches}

Matching is a **preview**: it may become a [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/oracleofthevoid)
supporter feature later. Looking up who has a card stays open to everyone.

**Trades** shows the players who have cards you want and want cards you have. The best ones come
first: players who match both ways (marked **two-way**), then those who have more of what you're
looking for. Each shows when they were last active, the cards on each side (with how many you want
or have) and their lists. Hover over a card for a look at it, or click it for its page.

Nobody listed yet? New lists open to trade all the time; look again later.

## Who has a card {#card}

On a card's page, the two arrows beside the public lists globe show how many players have the card
open to trade and how many want it (have/want). Click them for the players, under **Have it** and
**Want it**. You can look without logging in; you need to log in to message them.

## Messages {#messages}

**Message** on a trader opens a conversation with them; the first message notes the cards of the
trade, so you both know what it's about. The envelope in the menu bar shows how many messages you
haven't read, and **Messages** lists your conversations, newest first.

- You get an email when someone writes, once per conversation until you read it. It says who
  wrote, not what. Switch these emails off in Settings.
- **Mute** a player: their messages still arrive, with no email and no count.
- **Block** a player: neither of you can message the other until you unblock them.
- **Report** sends the conversation to the site admins.
- Switching off **Accept messages** in Settings stops new conversations. It also takes your lists
  off trading, since nobody could reach you.

A message holds up to 2,000 characters. To keep out spam, each player can start up to 20 new
conversations and send up to 200 messages a day.

## After a trade {#after}

Take the cards you traded away off your Have list, and the ones you got off your Want list (or
lower their counts). Matches come from your lists as they are, so a card left on them keeps
bringing traders to you for it.

## Gone quiet {#quiet}

So nobody waits on someone who has moved on, trade lists of players who haven't signed in for a
while drop out of the matches: after 30 days you get an email, and after 60 your lists are hidden
from other traders. Your lists stay as they are; sign in and they show again.
