# Trading and messages

Keep a list of the cards you want and a list of the ones you'd trade away, and the site finds the
players you can trade with. **Trades** (the two arrows in the menu bar) shows them; **Messages**
(the envelope) is where you talk it over.

## Opening a list to trade {#open}

1. Make a list of the cards you're after and set its type to **Want**, and one of the cards you'd
   trade away, type **Have**. Collections don't count: make a Have list of the cards you'd part with.
2. In the list view, set each one to **Open to trade**. That also makes the list Public.

You need a display name first (Settings), so traders know who you are, and **Accept messages**
switched on. Set a list back to **Not for trade** (or Private) to stop trading it.

A card on a Want list matches any printing of it, unless you picked a printing.

## Your matches {#matches}

**Trades** shows the players who have cards you want and want cards you have. The best ones come
first: players who match both ways (marked **two-way**), then those who have more of what you're
looking for. Each shows when they were last active, the cards on each side and their lists.

## Who has a card {#card}

On a card's page, the two arrows beside the public lists globe show how many players have the card
open to trade and how many want it (have/want). Click them for the players.

## Messages {#messages}

**Message** on a trader opens a conversation with them; the first message notes the cards of the
trade. The envelope in the menu bar shows how many messages you haven't read.

- You get an email when someone writes, once per conversation until you read it. It says who
  wrote, not what. Switch these emails off in Settings.
- **Mute** a player: their messages still arrive, with no email and no count.
- **Block** a player: neither of you can message the other until you unblock them.
- **Report** sends the conversation to the site admins.
- Switching off **Accept messages** in Settings stops new conversations. It also takes your lists
  off trading, since nobody could reach you.

## Gone quiet {#quiet}

So nobody waits on someone who has moved on, trade lists of players who haven't signed in for a
while drop out of the matches: after 30 days you get an email, and after 60 your lists are hidden
from other traders. Your lists stay as they are; sign in and they show again.
